## Overview

A boutique hospitality and property photography assignment documenting The Hut Hut, a secluded retreat nestled in Bingin, Uluwatu. The visual series captures the intimate cluster of six handcrafted wooden bungalows, the central turquoise swimming pool, open-air communal living spaces, and tranquil garden pathways tailored for peaceful coastal stays.

## Concept & Lighting

Highlighting the organic geometry of sustainable bamboo joinery, high-vaulted thatched ceilings, and fluid transitions between private suites and tropical outdoor living. Soft ambient daylight filtering through the surrounding canopy illuminates warm weathered teak surfaces, while low-angle morning sun accentuates tactile craftsmanship and serene island living.
